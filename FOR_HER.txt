Sourceter — renaming shop photos

WINDOWS
  Double-click Sourceter.exe. That's it, there's nothing to install.
  The first launch takes a few seconds; after that it's quick.

MAC
  Drag Sourceter.app into your Applications folder (or anywhere you like),
  then double-click it.
  The very first time only: right-click the app, choose Open, then click
  Open again in the box that appears. macOS asks this once for any app that
  didn't come from the App Store. After that, double-click works normally.

HOW TO USE IT
  Click the ? button in the top right corner. Six short steps.

  The very short version: put your photo folder on one side of the screen
  and Sourceter on the other, drag one shop's photos across, type the shop
  name, press Enter. Repeat for each shop, then press Confirm and rename.

  Nothing is renamed until you press Confirm, and you'll see the full list
  of old and new names before that happens.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
  Press "Undo a run" and pick the batch you want reversed. Every rename is
  written to a spreadsheet (_rename_log_....csv) in the same folder before
  a single file is touched, so the original names can always be recovered.
